# Privacy Policy — Lung Buddy

**Last updated:** 25 October 2026

Lung Buddy (“the App”) is a cut-down smoking tracker. This policy explains what we do with your information.

## Short version

- **No account.** You do not sign up.
- **Data stays on your phone.** Logs, budgets, streaks, challenges, and cosmetics are stored locally in an on-device database.
- **No ads. No analytics SDKs** in the current build.
- **No sale of personal data.**

## What we store (on your device only)

- Cigarette log timestamps and daily budgets you set
- Streak and challenge progress
- Pack price / cigs-per-pack for spend estimates
- Cosmetic unlocks and Plus flag (test unlock until Play Billing ships)
- Optional buddy display name

## What we do not collect

We do not currently collect name, email, precise location, contacts, or health records from other apps. The App is **not** a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## Sharing

The App does not upload your smoking log to our servers. If you use **Export data**, you choose where the JSON file goes (e.g. email or Files). That share is under your control.

## Home screen widget

The Android widget reads the same on-device summary (today’s count, budget, streak) so it can show on your launcher. It does not send that data off the device.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under the age required by your local laws for this kind of content.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes (for example when Play Billing or optional cloud features are added). The date at the top will change.

## Contact
