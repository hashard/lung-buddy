## App name

Lung Buddy

## Short description (≤ 80 characters)

Cut down with a goofy lung buddy — budgets, streaks, and chaos cosmetics.

## Full description

Lung Buddy helps you **cut down** on smoking with a light, cartoon vibe — not shame, not lectures.

**How it works**
• Set a daily budget and tap **I SMOKED** when you light up  
• Undo same-day oops taps  
• Watch your lung buddy darken and cough as you climb toward (or past) budget  
• Keep streaks by finishing under-budget days  
• Take optional quests for silly cosmetic loot  
• See today’s spend from your pack price  

**Lung Buddy Plus** (coming to billing)
• Unlocks the whole closet  
• Stats, weekly money recap, and home-screen widget  

**Privacy-first**
• No account  
• Data stays on your phone  
• Export or wipe anytime  

Not medical advice. Just a buddy with lungs and opinions.
