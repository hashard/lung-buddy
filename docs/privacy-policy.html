Privacy Policy — Lung Buddy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Lung Buddy (“the App”) is a cut-down smoking tracker. This page explains what we do with your information.

Short version

  • No account. You do not sign up.
  • Data stays on your phone. Logs, budgets, streaks, challenges, and cosmetics are stored locally.
  • No ads. No analytics SDKs in the current build.
  • No sale of personal data.

What we store (on your device only)

  • Cigarette log timestamps and daily budgets you set
  • Streak and challenge progress
  • Pack price / cigs-per-pack for spend estimates
  • Cosmetic unlocks and Plus flag
  • Optional buddy display name

What we do not collect

We do not currently collect name, email, precise location, contacts, or health records from other apps. The App is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Sharing

The App does not upload your smoking log to our servers. If you use Export data, you choose where the JSON file goes.

Home screen widget

The Android widget reads the same on-device summary so it can show on your launcher. It does not send that data off the device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
